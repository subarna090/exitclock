The problem

The facts are public. They're just scattered, dated, and easy to find three weeks too late.

Almost everything written about layoffs in India is either American advice that doesn't apply, or a ₹99 PDF assembled from blog posts.

The four Labour Codes

The four Labour Codes came into force in November 2025 and introduced a common definition of "wages" with a 50% allowance rule that can change the base your gratuity is calculated on. An old salary-structure calculator can now give you the wrong number.

Verified: Sept 2026 · source: SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER

₹20 lakh gratuity cap

Gratuity is generally 15 days' wages for each completed year, currently capped at ₹20 lakh — and fixed-term employees became eligible at one year, not five, on contract expiry.

Verified: Sept 2026 · source: SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER

10% PF TDS

A taxable PF withdrawal before five years of continuous service can attract 10% TDS under section 192A above ₹50,000. Service with a previous employer can count, if the PF was transferred.

Verified: Sept 2026 · source: SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER

₹25 lakh leave ceiling

Leave encashment for non-government employees is exempt only up to a statutory ceiling currently at ₹25 lakh, on a lower-of calculation — and whether you can encash at all is set by your employer's policy, which is a separate question.

Verified: Sept 2026 · source: SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER

Group medical cover

Group medical cover can end on your last working day, at month end, or on some other policy-defined date. Not, as most people assume, at the end of a severance period.

Why I built this

Someone close to me signed a settlement without asking for a component-wise breakdown. By the time we realised what was missing, the people who could have fixed it had stopped replying.

Then I started seeing the same gaps everywhere — missed deadlines, benefits ending without warning, and people accepting full-and-final figures they couldn't actually verify.

I got tired of explaining the same things over WhatsApp. So I looked for something India-specific to share. What I found was either American advice that didn't apply here or ₹99 PDFs stitched together from blogs.

The real answers exist — buried in government portals and written in language nobody wants to decode during the worst week of their working life.

So I built this to make those answers simple, practical, and usable when people actually need them.

What I am, and what I am not

I am not a lawyer, a chartered accountant, an insurance adviser or a financial planner. Nothing here is advice about your specific situation. What this kit does is narrower: it tells you which questions to ask, which deadlines start when, and where the official rule actually lives — so that when you do talk to HR, or a CA, or a lawyer, you already know what you're asking about.

Honest answers

The questions people actually ask.

Is this legal or tax advice?
No. It is dated educational material built from official sources, with every claim marked with the date it was verified. It tells you what to ask and where the rule lives, so that when you do speak to HR or a professional, you know what you are asking about.
Will it get me a job?
No, and be careful of anything that says otherwise. It will make your search measurable and your evidence stronger. The market does the rest.
Is it current?
It is the India Edition, September 2026, and it will not be right forever. Rules change. The changelog is public, every regulatory claim carries a verification date, and buyers get corrections by email for twelve months. Read the changelog.
I'm not in tech. Is this useful?
Yes. The money, benefits, runway and family modules apply to any salaried professional. Four of the ten role packs are non-engineering.
Why not just ask ChatGPT?
Ask it what date your gratuity is due and it will give you a confident answer without asking which year you joined, whether you are fixed-term, or which wage base your employer used. This kit is a sequence with the sources attached — and a tool that does the date arithmetic for your actual exit.

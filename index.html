Honest answers

The questions people actually ask.

Is this legal or tax advice?
No. It's dated educational material built from official sources, with every claim marked with the date it was verified. It tells you what to ask and where the rule lives — so when you speak to HR or a professional, you know what you're asking about.
Will it get me a job?
No, and be careful of anything that says otherwise. It will make your search measurable and your evidence stronger. The market does the rest.
Why not just ask ChatGPT?
Ask it when your gratuity is due and you'll get a confident answer without it ever asking which year you joined, whether you're fixed-term, or which wage base your employer used. This is a sequence with the sources attached — and a tool that does the date arithmetic for your actual exit.
I'm not in tech. Is it still useful?
Yes. The money, benefits, runway and family modules apply to any salaried professional in India. Four of the ten role packs are non-engineering.

It's the India Edition, September 2026, written against the four Labour Codes in force since 21 November 2025. Rules change — the changelog is public, every regulatory claim carries a verification date, and buyers get corrections by email for twelve months. Read the changelog.

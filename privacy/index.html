Privacy

Your exit schedule stays in your browser.

Short version: the free tool does not send dates, salaries, names or family details to a server.

What the tool stores

The Exit Clock computes its schedule in your browser. It does not require an account. If your browser offers local storage, it may save the fields you entered on your own device so the schedule is still there when you return. You can clear that data from the tool or your browser settings.

What the tool does not collect

  • Your last working day, salary, service history, health-cover date or runway are not sent to Exit Clock by the free tool.
  • The site does not use advertising cookies.
  • The site does not use Google Analytics.

Email schedule requests

The email form is wired to a single endpoint placeholder until a publishing endpoint is configured. When it is connected, the email address will be used to send the schedule and, only where you opt in, related Exit Clock updates. The schedule itself remains computed in your browser.

Product purchases

Checkout, payment processing and download delivery are handled by the selected checkout provider. Their privacy terms apply to payment information.

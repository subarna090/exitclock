Your schedule

Every date here is a prompt to ask, not a guarantee. Rules, portals and employer policies change, and your contract may give you better terms than the statutory minimum. Where a date matters — a signature, a withdrawal, a policy lapse — verify it against the current official source before you act.

Public holidays are not built into the working-day counts. Check the calendar for your state before you treat a two-day deadline as expired.

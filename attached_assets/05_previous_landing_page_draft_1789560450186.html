The problem

None of this is secret. It's just scattered, dated, and easy to find three weeks too late.

Most of what's written about layoffs in India is American advice that doesn't apply, or a ₹99 PDF assembled from blog posts. The actual checkable facts sit on government portals, in language nobody reads during the worst week of their working life.

Nov 2025 The four Labour Codes came into force, introducing a common definition of "wages" with a 50% allowance rule that can change the base your gratuity is calculated on. An old salary-structure calculator can now give you the wrong number.
₹20 lakh The current cap on gratuity, generally paid at 15 days' wages per completed year. Fixed-term employees became eligible at one year on contract expiry, not five.
10% TDS A taxable PF withdrawal before five years of continuous service can attract TDS under section 192A above ₹50,000. Service with a previous employer can count — if the PF was transferred.
₹25 lakh The statutory ceiling on leave-encashment exemption for non-government employees, on a lower-of calculation. Whether you can encash at all is set by your employer's policy — a separate question people routinely merge with this one.
Day one Group medical cover can end on your last working day, at month end, or on another policy-defined date. Not, as most people assume, at the end of a severance period.

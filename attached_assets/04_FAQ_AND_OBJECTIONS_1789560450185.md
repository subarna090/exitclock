# Buyer FAQ and Objection Handling

## Is this only for people already laid off?
No. One of the two primary paths is specifically for people who are still employed but want to prepare before access, benefits or income change.

## Is this only for software engineers?
No. V1 contains 10 persona packs spanning technical and non-technical roles.

## Does this guarantee a job?
No. It provides a structured readiness and career-recovery system; hiring outcomes depend on the market, role fit, evidence, interviews and many external factors.

## Is the PF/gratuity/insurance section legal or tax advice?
No. It is a dated, India-first educational guide based on official sources available for the edition. High-stakes decisions should be verified against current rules and, when appropriate, qualified professionals.

## Why not just use ChatGPT?
The product is a curated sequence: what to secure before a layoff, what to do in the first 72 hours, what financial and benefit questions to track, how to calculate runway, how to choose a target, how to build role-specific AI evidence and how to run the next 90 days. The included prompts are supporting tools, not the product itself.

## Is there a support group?
The product works standalone. A moderated community can be offered as a separate tier once the seller has created and staffed an actual channel. Never advertise a group that does not yet exist.
